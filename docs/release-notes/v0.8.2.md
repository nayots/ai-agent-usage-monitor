## Highlights

**The tray icon no longer gets stuck on one provider.** Since v0.5.0, if any provider hit its
limit or started failing, the tray icon would turn to it and hold there — by design, so you
wouldn't miss it while looking away. But with only one provider in trouble, that "hold" never let
go: every healthy provider disappeared from the tray for as long as the trouble lasted, which
looked identical to rotation having stopped. It now keeps cycling through every provider, healthy
or troubled alike.

---

## What broke

Tray rotation picked its next frame from a restricted set: whenever any provider was at its limit
or failing, only those troubled providers were eligible for a turn. With exactly one troubled
provider, that set had one member — nothing to alternate with — so rotation stopped and the icon
sat on that single provider indefinitely, even with two other providers reading normally.

## What changed

Every provider now takes its turn in rotation regardless of state. Nothing seizes the icon for
good anymore, and healthy providers are never excluded from the turn order even while another
provider is in trouble. Turning rotation off entirely still settles the icon on the worst provider
rather than an arbitrary one — that part of the v0.5.0 behaviour is unchanged.

## Notes

- This affects the **tray icon only** — the widget window and mini strip were never affected.
- The single-troubled-provider case was the one that froze outright. With two or more providers
  in trouble at once, rotation was already moving between them before this fix — it just left the
  healthy ones out, which this fix also corrects.
